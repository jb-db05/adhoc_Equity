A cheat sheet for the generalist

Student housing companies are judged on a handful of property metrics that differ from those used for industrial or consumer companies. The table explains the main ones, why they matter and where Xior stands.

MetricWhat it measuresWhy it mattersXior today
OccupancyShare of beds that are let.Drives revenue. Beds are let once a year, for the academic year, so the summer letting season sets most of the next twelve months.98.5% at 30 Sept 2026
Like-for-like rental growthRent growth on the same buildings, excluding purchases and sales.Measures pricing power; the test is whether it beats inflation.+4.62% year on year; at least 4.4% guided for 2026
EPRA earnings per shareRecurring profit: rents less costs, interest and tax, excluding property revaluations and hedging gains or losses.The relevant earnings figure for a property company; reported net profit swings with valuations.EUR 2.30 guided for 2026, EUR 2.40 for 2027
Payout ratio and dividend yieldShare of EPRA earnings paid as dividend; dividend divided by the share price.Belgian REITs must pay out most of their earnings, so the dividend is the main return. A payout near 100% leaves no cushion.80% payout; EUR 1.84 per share, 7.2% yield
EPRA NTA per shareNet tangible assets: appraised property value less debt, excluding deferred tax and hedges.The property company's book value, based on independent valuations.EUR 38.69 at 30 Sept 2026
Premium or discount to NTAShare price against NTA per share.The market's view of the valuations, the debt and the growth. A deep discount makes new shares costly to issue.-34%
Valuation yieldAnnual rent divided by the property's value, as used by valuers.Small changes move values a lot: on a 5% yield, a rise of 0.25 point cuts values by about 5%.Values +0.95% in 2026 to Sept
Loan-to-value (LTV)Net debt divided by the value of the properties.The key balance-sheet risk: the same debt on lower values gives a higher LTV.51.31%; target below 50%
Debt ratioBelgian legal measure of debt to total assets for REITs.The legal ceiling is 65%; banks and investors want a wide margin below it.51.42%
Cost of debt and hedge ratioAverage interest rate paid; share of debt fixed or swapped, and for how long.Shows how quickly higher market rates feed into earnings.3.05%; 89% hedged for 4.6 years (Q1 2026)
Provision ratePurpose-built beds as a share of full-time students in a city or country.A low rate signals room for new supply, and a shortage that supports rents.Low on the continent against the UK
Development pipelineProjects under construction or committed.Adds future rent, but must be paid for with debt, equity or disposals.EUR 171m active
Market value and liquidityShare price times shares; value of shares traded each day.Small, thinly traded stocks are harder to buy or sell in size, and can be left out by large funds.About EUR 1.2bn; about EUR 1.4m a day
Sources: Syz Research, Bloomberg, company data
