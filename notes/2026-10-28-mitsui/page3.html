READING THE NUMBERS

The FY3/27 plan implies profit of about JPY 626 billion over the last nine months of the year, after JPY 294.1 billion in the first quarter. This is below the first-quarter run rate. The difference reflects one-off gains in the first quarter and the company's commodity and currency assumptions (Syz calculation). How commodity prices and the yen have moved since the plan was set will largely decide whether it is revised on 4 November.
