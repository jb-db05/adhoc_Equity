MITSUI: WHAT THE PLAN IMPLIES

The JPY 920 billion plan implies about JPY 626 billion over the last nine months, below the first-quarter pace. That quarter included one-off gains on investments. Each USD 1 per tonne on iron ore moves annual profit by about JPY 3 billion, and each JPY 1 against the dollar by about JPY 4.6 billion.

MARUBENI: WHAT THE PLAN IMPLIES

The JPY 580 billion plan implies about JPY 394 billion over the last nine months, also below the first-quarter pace. On current guidance, return on equity would reach about 13% this year, so the 15% goal needs higher profits or more buybacks. New copper volumes from Centinela are expected from 2027.

Where they differ

  • Commodity mix. Mitsui is more exposed to iron ore and energy, where prices could soften in 2027 and 2028. Marubeni is more exposed to copper, where supply remains tight.
  • Return framework. Mitsui has a dividend floor of JPY 140 to FY3/29 and returns around half of cumulative core operating cash flow. Marubeni has raised its GC2027 return target to JPY 900 billion.
  • Valuation. Mitsui trades at a slightly higher multiple of its own plan. Marubeni trades well above its 10-year valuation range on consensus earnings (Chart 4).
