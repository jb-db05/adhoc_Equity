1. What the company does

Prudential sells life, health and savings products in Asia and Africa, through agents and more than 180 partner banks. Its key markets include Hong Kong, mainland China through CITIC Prudential Life, Thailand, Taiwan and India.

2. Why it could do well

Outside mainland China, new business profit grew 13% in the third quarter of 2026, driven by Hong Kong, Thailand and Taiwan, and the bank channel grew by more than 20%. Over nine months, group new business profit reached USD 2.09bn, up 6% at constant exchange rates. The group targets 15 to 20% annual growth in new business profit from 2022 to 2027.

3. What sets it apart

Few insurers combine leading positions in so many Asian markets with a bank network of this size. The planned purchase of a 75% controlling stake in Bharti Life, subject to regulatory approval, would strengthen the group in India.

4. Is the share cheap?

The market value is about GBP 23.5bn. Management targets at least USD 4.4bn of operating free surplus generation in 2027, the cash available for growth and shareholder returns, and plans USD 1.5bn of buybacks by the end of 2026. Delivery on the 2027 target is the main support for the valuation; a shortfall would remove the key argument.

5. Main risks

China could weigh for longer: management expects new business profit there to fall 10 to 15% in 2026 and now sees group 2026 guidance as more stretching than in August. Group new business profit grew only 1% in the third quarter. The Bharti Life deal still needs approval, and reliance on banks makes the group sensitive to distribution rules.

6. What to watch next

The full-year 2026 results: growth outside China and the size of the China decline. Any sign that bank-channel sales in China stabilise. Completion of the buyback and of the Bharti Life acquisition, and progress towards the USD 4.4bn target.

Conclusion

Prudential could suit an investor seeking long-term exposure to Asian growth through a single stock. The China setback is real but limited, and the rest of the group is growing at a double-digit rate. The full-year results will show whether the 2027 targets remain within reach.
