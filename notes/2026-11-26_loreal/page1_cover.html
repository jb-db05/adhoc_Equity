Faster growth, and a share price that has caught up

Growth has accelerated

L'Oréal's sales reached EUR 11.56bn in the third quarter of 2026, up 11.9% as reported and 7.2% like-for-like. Adjusted for the timing effects of its IT transformation, growth was 7.7%, against 6.5% in the first half and a global beauty market that the company estimates grew about 5%. The gap with reported growth reflects acquisitions, led by Kering Beauté (including Creed) since April, and currencies.

The improvement is broad. All four divisions grew. Professional Products and Dermatological Beauty (La Roche-Posay, CeraVe) grew at double-digit rates, Luxe by about 7% with fragrance as the main driver, and Consumer Products by about 4%. South Asia Pacific, the Middle East and Africa grew 19.2%, with India and Indonesia accelerating, and mainland China grew at a low double-digit rate. North America was the soft spot, at 2.2% like-for-like.

Profitability is holding up. In the first half the operating margin rose 20 basis points to a record 21.3%, even as spending on brand support increased.

The share price has moved first

The shares rose 5.6% on the day after the third-quarter release. At EUR 443.35 they are up 21% since the start of the year, against 8% for the Stoxx Europe 600, and close to their record close of EUR 456.90 from May 2024.

The longer view is less flattering. Over five years L'Oréal has gained 10%, against 37% for the European market, as its valuation fell from more than 40 times expected earnings in 2021. This year's gain splits roughly in half: consensus earnings for the next twelve months are up about 10% (partly because the twelve-month window rolls forward), and the multiple rose from 26.8 to 29.3 times.

What this means for investors

L'Oréal again grows faster than its market, with record margins. At 29 times expected earnings the shares trade in line with their ten-year median but at the top of the 24 to 30 times range of the last two years, so much of the acceleration is already in the price. From here, further gains would likely need earnings upgrades rather than a higher multiple. Full-year results in February 2027 and the start of the Gucci licence in mid-2027 are the next milestones.
