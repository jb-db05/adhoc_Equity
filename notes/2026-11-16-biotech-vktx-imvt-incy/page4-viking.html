1. What the company does

Viking develops VK2735, a dual GLP-1/GIP agonist for obesity, the same mechanism as the market leader's tirzepatide. The injectable version is in two Phase 3 trials (VANQUISH-1 in obesity, about 4,500 patients, and VANQUISH-2 in obesity with type 2 diabetes, about 1,000 patients), both fully enrolled. An oral version entered Phase 3 (SUNRISE) in October, with data expected in 2028.

2. Why it could do well

Obesity is one of the largest drug markets ever built, and payers and patients want more suppliers. A positive VANQUISH readout would make Viking one of very few independent companies with a late-stage dual agonist, in both injectable and oral form. Its small size relative to that opportunity has also made it a recurring subject of takeover speculation, although this should not be the investment case.

3. What sets it apart

In a Phase 2 maintenance study, patients who switched from weekly injections to every-other-week or monthly doses kept 82% to 97% of their weight loss. Less frequent dosing could be a real point of difference. An earlier-stage amylin programme (VK3019) adds a second mechanism, with a multiple-dose study planned for the first quarter of 2027.

4. Cheap, or a value trap?

An enterprise value of about USD 2.8 bn (Bloomberg) looks small next to the obesity market, and the gap to Immunovant shows how much doubt is priced in. That doubt is rational: Viking would arrive years after the two leaders, into a market where prices are falling and oral drugs from the incumbents are reaching patients. Being as good as existing drugs may not be enough.

5. Main risks

The first risk is the Phase 3 result itself, including tolerability and drop-out rates, which hurt the shares after the 2025 oral Phase 2 study. The second is funding: at the third-quarter loss rate, cash covers about five quarters (Syz calculation), taking the company to around the 2027 readout. A further equity raise or a partnership would very likely be needed before any launch. The third is commercial: Viking has no sales force and would face competitors with far greater manufacturing and marketing scale.

6. What to watch next

Part 2 of the maintenance study (2027), the VK3019 dose study, any partnering news, and above all the VANQUISH data in the second half of 2027.

Conclusion

Viking offers the highest potential reward of the three, but also the most concentrated risk: one drug, one readout window and a funding need. The profile stays binary until the VANQUISH data, and takeover hopes alone do not justify the share.
